Who runs this site

      
This is the personal portfolio of Ludovic Lachance, a Finance and Accounting student based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. It is not a business and nothing is sold here. For any question about this site or these policies, email llach097@uottawa.ca.

    


    

      
Privacy policy

      
What I collect

      
Nothing. This site has no accounts, no sign-up or contact forms, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. I never see who visits.

      
What your browser can remember (only if you say yes)

      
If you choose Remember in the storage notice, your browser keeps two small notes on your own device: which sections you have explored, and that you have already seen the welcome card. They never leave your device and are never sent to me or anyone else. Your yes/no answer itself is also kept so the site doesn't ask again. Details and a delete button are under Cookies & storage.

      
Hosting

      
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. I don't receive those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

      
Fonts and code

      
The fonts and the 3D library are served from this site itself, so loading the page doesn't contact any other company's servers.

      
If you email me

      
I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use them only to reply. I don't add you to any mailing list and I don't share your message.

      
Links to other sites

      
Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, the Canadian Volleyball League and PDF files open other pages; those sites have their own privacy policies.

      
Your rights

      
Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and, for visitors from the EU or UK, the GDPR give you the right to ask what personal information I hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Since the site collects nothing, this would only ever concern an email you sent me. Just email me and I'll take care of it.

      
This site is not directed at children. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Accessibility

      
I want everyone to be able to read this portfolio, and I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

      
    
        
  • A text version has every section on one plain page and works with screen readers. A "skip to the text version" link is the first thing you reach with the keyboard.
    • 
        
  • Everything works with the keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to walk, Enter to open, Esc to go back, Tab to move between buttons, and 1 to 8 to jump to a section.
    • 
        
  • Text is white on dark green for strong contrast; photos have descriptions (alt text); buttons have clear labels.
    • 
        
  • Animations in the interface are turned off if your device is set to reduce motion.
    • 
      

      
The 3D city itself is visual and can't be fully described to a screen reader, which is why the text version has all the same content. If something doesn't work for you, please email llach097@uottawa.ca and I'll fix it.
